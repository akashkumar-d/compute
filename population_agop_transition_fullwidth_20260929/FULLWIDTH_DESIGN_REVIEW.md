It is not approved for dispatch or training by this design review.** Review is based on the parent's stated design; the new bundle was not yet available. No model imports, evaluations, network calls or extra agents were used.

The count is exact: each of two states has one gradient base plus four central-difference evaluations at each of orders12 and16, then one gradient evaluation at order16/limit10 compared with the existing order16/limit12 base. No trajectory updates, additional cache checks, old Euler proposals, or Armijo steps belong in this count.

For each order, form the same declared weighted direction from that order's full gradient, with head_lr applied once. If the prior probe's Euclidean direction normalization is retained, use the normalized direction consistently in both the nominal slope and the two central differences. The derivative sign remains `[L(theta-epsilon*D)-L(theta+epsilon*D)]/(2*epsilon)`. Pin the two epsilon fractions and scale convention in the new source/manifest; this review does not silently choose or change them. Order-specific directions can differ, so compare base losses, gradients, teacher terms, Gram symmetry and directional mismatches separately. A relative-only slope threshold needs its small-slope/roundoff qualification retained.

Order12 versus16 and limit10 versus12 are finite convergence diagnostics. They do not bound the infinite-domain quadrature error. Successful full-width checks at these two states would strengthen numerical evidence there; they would not certify intervening states, a training trajectory, AGOP/refit diagnostics, early plateau success, or any proposed mechanism change.
